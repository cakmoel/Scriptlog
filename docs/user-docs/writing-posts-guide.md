# How to Write Posts in scriptLog
### A Beginner-Friendly Guide

---

## Introduction

Welcome to scriptLog! This guide will teach you how to create, edit, and manage blog posts and categories in your admin panel. Don't worry—we'll walk you through each step.

---

## Quick Overview

| What You Want to Do | Where to Go |
|---------------------|-------------|
| Write a new post | **Posts** → **Add New** |
| Edit an existing post | **Posts** → Click the ✏️ button |
| Delete a post | **Posts** → Click the 🗑️ button |
| Create a category | **Categories** → **Add New** |
| Edit a category | **Categories** → Click the ✏️ button |

---

## Part 1: Writing Your First Post

### Step 1: Go to Posts

1. Log in to your admin panel
2. Find **Posts** in the left sidebar
3. Click it to open the Posts page

You will see a table listing all your posts.

### Step 2: Click "Add New"

On the Posts page, look for a blue button that says **Add New** in the top-right corner. Click it.

This opens the Post Editor where you will write your content.

### Step 3: Fill in Your Post Details

The editor has two main areas. Let's break them down:

#### Left Side - Your Content

| Field | What to Enter | Tips |
|-------|---------------|------|
| **Title** | Your post headline | Keep it under 200 characters |
| **Content** | Your main article | Use the toolbar to format text |
| **Meta Description** | Short summary for search engines | Keep it under 320 characters |
| **Featured Post** | Choose "Yes" or "No" | Select "Yes" for important posts |
| **Publication Date** | When to publish | Leave empty to publish now |
| **Post Visibility** | Who can see it | Public, Private, or Password Protected |
| **Language** | Select language | Choose your post's language |

#### Right Side - Publishing Options

| Field | What to Do |
|-------|------------|
| **Categories** | Check the boxes for your post's categories |
| **Tags** | Type keywords separated by commas |
| **Featured Image** | Click "Select Image" to add a picture |
| **Post Status** | Choose: Publish, Draft, Pending, or Private |
| **Comment Status** | Choose: Open (allow comments) or Closed |

### Step 4: Click Publish

When you are ready:

- **First time?**: Click the **Publish** button (paper plane icon)
- **Editing existing?**: Click the **Update** button (save icon)

Your post is now live!

## Part 3: Managing Categories

Categories help organize your posts into groups.

### Creating a Category

1. Click **Categories** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add New** (blue button, top-right)
3. Enter a **Title** (e.g., "Technology", "Travel")
4. Set **Active Status**:
   - **Yes** = category is visible
   - **No** = category is hidden
5. Select **Language** (optional)
6. Click **Add Category**

### Editing a Category

1. Go to **Categories**
2. Find the category you want to edit
3. Click the ✏️ button
4. Make your changes
5. Click **Update**

### Deleting a Category

1. Go to **Categories**
2. Find the category
3. Click the 🗑️ button
4. Confirm "Yes" to delete

**Note**: Deleting a category does not delete its posts. Posts become "uncategorized."

## Part 4: Managing Existing Posts

### Viewing All Posts

Go to **Posts** — you'll see a table with:

| Column | What It Shows |
|--------|----------------|
| # | Row number |
| Title | Your post headline |
| Author | Who wrote it |
| Date | When it was published |
| Edit | Button to modify |
| Delete | Button to remove |

### Editing a Post

1. Go to **Posts**
2. Click the ✏️ button on any post
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Update**

### Deleting a Post

1. Go to **Posts**
2. Click the 🗑️ button
3. A message appears: "Are you sure want to delete Post '[title]'?"
4. Click **OK** to delete, or **Cancel** to keep it

**Warning**: Deleted posts cannot be recovered!

## Part 5: Post Status Explained

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|----------------|
| **Publish** | Everyone can see it |
| **Draft** | Work in progress, not visible |
| **Pending** | Waiting for review |
| **Private** | Only logged-in users can see |

## Part 6: Visibility Options

| Option | Who Can See It |
|--------|----------------|
| **Public** | Anyone |
| **Private** | Only logged-in users |
| **Password Protected** | Only people with the password |

If you choose **Password Protected**, a field appears for you to enter the password.

## Part 7: Common Tasks

### Add a Featured Image

1. In the post editor, find **Featured Image** on the right
2. Click **Select Image**
3. Browse your Media Library or upload a new image
4. Click the image you want
5. Click **Set as Featured Image**

### Add Tags

1. Find the **Tags** field on the right
2. Type your tags separated by commas
   - Example: `tutorial, beginners, how-to`
3. Tags help readers find related content

### Schedule a Post

1. In the **Publication Date** field
2. Click the calendar icon
3. Pick a future date and time
4. Set status to **Publish**
5. The post will go live automatically at that time

### Backdate a Post

1. In the **Publication Date** field
2. Enter a past date
3. The post will appear as if it was published on that date

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I save my post as a draft?**
A: Yes! Set the Post Status to "Draft" and your work will be saved.

**Q: How do I edit a post?**
A: Go to Posts, find your post, and click the Edit (✏️) button.

**Q: What happens if I delete a post?**
A: It's permanent. The post and its comments are deleted forever.

**Q: Can I have multiple categories on one post?**
A: Yes! Check as many category boxes as you need.

**Q: How do I allow comments on my post?**
A: Set "Comment Status" to **Open** in the Publishing Options.
